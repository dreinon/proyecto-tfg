# Publication basis for selected professional-pilot figures

**Status:** current operational record, 6 September 2026  
**Applies to:** the twelve predeclared external cases embedded in the openly accessible TFG and its defence materials  
**Does not alter:** the frozen experimental sample, outputs, metrics, or review decisions

## Authorization and provenance

The professional-pilot pages come from the music archive of the Societat Joventut Musical
d'Albal (SJMA). The student compiled the corpus with institutional access and, acting as SJMA's
president and legal representative, has confirmed the entity's express authorization to use and
reproduce the twelve supplied test pages and their HR/LR/SR derivatives in this TFG and its defence
materials.

This institutional authorization does not mean that the student owns the underlying compositions,
arrangements, editions, engravings, or other third-party rights, and it is not described as a
donation or transfer of ownership. The conservative `private-study-only` value in the frozen source
manifest records the status at the time the experiment was fixed; it is retained for provenance
rather than rewritten after seeing results. This later authorization supersedes that operational
restriction only for the bounded in-thesis uses stated here.

## Open-access publication boundary

The thesis is intended for open institutional deposit. It reproduces the complete predeclared
twelve-case external qualitative set so that every individual decision can be audited, rather than
showing only favourable examples. These twelve test pages were selected from the sixteen supplied
pages before opening any model output. Each reproduction:

- is embedded in an academic analysis rather than offered as a standalone score;
- represents one instrumental page from a larger band work, not the performing materials for the
  complete work;
- identifies the credited work and composer or arranger, the instrumental part, SJMA as the source
  archive, the processing condition, and the fact that LR/SR panels are TFG transformations;
- is limited to the resolution and extent needed to inspect the claimed image behaviour; and
- is accompanied by critical commentary explaining both what the model improves and what it fails
  to recover.

For SMB material, the dataset's stated CC BY-NC 4.0 licence, attribution, non-commercial academic
purpose, and indication of transformations form the publication basis, without assuming that the
dataset card settles every third-party right in every score. For the selected SJMA material, the
record combines the institution's express authorization with the narrowly justified academic
analysis and citation purpose. The latter is not presented as a general licence for the underlying
work.

## Uses not authorized by this record

- Publishing or committing the raw sixteen-page corpus, the complete 216-output pilot, or the
  private review bundle.
- Releasing the pages as a public dataset, training corpus, or reusable image collection.
- Standalone redistribution, public deployment, or commercial exploitation of the source pages.
- Claiming that an SR derivative is an authoritative edition, a faithful replacement for the
  source, or evidence of ownership by the student or SJMA of third-party works.

Raw files and non-selected derivatives stay outside Git. Any broader use requires a new rights
review and authorization appropriate to that use.

## Administrative decision

ETSINF Secretariat informed the student on 6 September 2026 that changes requiring CAT approval
could no longer be processed before the September defence and offered closed access as the
available confidentiality route. The student has chosen not to request that restriction and to
retain the ordinary open-deposit route, because the thesis does not publish the corpus as a dataset
and the selected reproductions have the bounded bases and attribution described above. The original
communication and the institutional authorization are retained privately as administrative
evidence.

Open access is not treated as proof of ownership or as a substitute for permission, attribution,
or proportionality. If Ebrón or ETSINF requires a specific declaration during deposit, the live
screen and the exact administrative instruction take precedence over this working record.
